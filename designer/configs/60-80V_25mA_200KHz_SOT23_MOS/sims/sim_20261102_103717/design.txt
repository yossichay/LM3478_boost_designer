Notes
- 60 V: load step 5 mA → 25 mA deviates about 162 mV (ΔI / (2π·fc·Cout)).
- 80 V: load step 5 mA → 25 mA deviates about 279 mV (ΔI / (2π·fc·Cout)).
- Estimated efficiency at 80 V / 25 mA: about 93% (loss table in the report / Losses tab; MOSFET and diode values: entered or the budget targets).
- 60 V: CCM above 13.9 mA at Vin 12 V; pulse skipping below about 90.3 µA (325 ns minimum on-time).
- 80 V: CCM above 11.1 mA at Vin 12 V; pulse skipping below about 64 µA (325 ns minimum on-time).
- The loop model ignores inductor DCR and capacitor ESL; it uses the AN-1286 resistive load.
