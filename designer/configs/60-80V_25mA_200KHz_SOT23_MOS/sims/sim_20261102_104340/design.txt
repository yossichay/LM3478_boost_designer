Notes
- Currents are sized with the estimated efficiency 92%, not the 95% entered (loss estimate below).
- 60 V: load step 5 mA → 15 mA deviates about 92 mV (ΔI / (2π·fc·Cout)).
- 80 V: load step 5 mA → 15 mA deviates about 158 mV (ΔI / (2π·fc·Cout)).
- Estimated efficiency at 80 V / 15 mA: about 92% (loss table in the report / Losses tab; MOSFET and diode values: entered or the budget targets).
- 60 V: CCM above 9.88 mA at Vin 12 V; pulse skipping below about 64.3 µA (325 ns minimum on-time).
- 80 V: CCM above 7.88 mA at Vin 12 V; pulse skipping below about 45.5 µA (325 ns minimum on-time).
- The loop model ignores inductor DCR and capacitor ESL; it uses the AN-1286 resistive load.

WARNINGS
